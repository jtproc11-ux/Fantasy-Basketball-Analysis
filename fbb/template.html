How the projection works

Points per minute

  • This season's fantasy points per minute, blended with last season's rate. Last season counts as 150 minutes of evidence, so small samples like Missi's 2 games get pulled toward his normal level.
  • A 20% nudge toward the last 5 games, to catch real improvement.

Minutes

  • 50% last 3 games, 20% last 5, 30% season. Minutes are the clearest sign of a role change, so recent games count the most.
  • Projected FP/G = points per minute × projected minutes.

Rest-of-season total

  • FP/G × team games left × expected availability: 92% for healthy players (normal missed games) and 75% for Missi, who is out with a knee bone bruise and has no return date.
  • Limits: there's no injury feed beyond the game logs, no news on depth charts, and nothing from NBA.com, which blocks cloud servers. Check news before you make a move.
